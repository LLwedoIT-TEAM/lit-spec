# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and artifacts rather than people.
- Respect artistic intent, authorship, consent, privacy, and rights.
- Welcome clarifying questions and good-faith disagreement.
- Avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Step back when a maintainer asks a discussion to cool down or move channels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, spam, deliberate disruption, and sustained bad-faith participation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and documented when privacy and safety allow.

For immediate platform abuse or threats, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). To request private contact with the project maintainer, open a public issue containing only the request and no sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities must use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers must protect the privacy and safety of people who report conduct concerns. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an enforcement process is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the LIT project.
